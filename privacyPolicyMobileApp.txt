**Privacy Policy for ROS2Sense**

**Last Updated:** 13 March 2025

---

### **1. Introduction**
Welcome to ROS2Sense ("we," "our," or "us"). This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application ROS2Sense (the "App").

---

### **2. Information We Collect**

#### **2.1 Sensor Data**
Our app collects data from various device sensors, including:
- Accelerometer
- Gyroscope
- Magnetometer
- GPS/Location
- Ambient Light Sensor
- Pressure Sensor (few mobile, e.g., Samsung S4)
- Temperature Sensor (few mobile, e.g., Samsung S4)
- Humidity Sensor (few mobile, e.g., Samsung S4)
- Camera Front & Back
- Flash
- Microphone
- Speaker
- Fingerprint/Face ID

#### **2.2 How We Use Sensor Data**
- All sensor data is processed locally on your device.
- Data is used for ROS2 (Robot Operating System 2) communication.
- No sensor data is stored permanently on your device.
- No sensor data is transmitted to external servers except when explicitly sharing with ROS2 nodes.
- **Camera** access transmits raw image data to ROS2 nodes exclusively through user-triggered ROS actions/services. No on-device processing or storage occurs.
- **GPS** location data is streamed to ROS2 nodes only when activated by user-defined ROS commands. Location information is not processed or retained locally.
- **Fingerprint/Face ID** biometric verification is executed through ROS2-authenticated actions initiated by user triggers. Biometric data is never stored or transfered.

---

### **3. Data Storage and Transmission**

#### **3.1 Local Storage**
- The app stores minimal configuration data locally.
- No sensitive personal information is stored.
- All sensor readings are temporary and cleared when the app is closed.

#### **3.2 Data Transmission**
- Data is only transmitted to ROS2 nodes on your local network.
- No data is sent to our servers.
- All network communication is confined to your local network.
- We do not collect or transmit any personal information.

---

### **4. Permissions**
The app requires the following permissions:
- **Camera:** Temporary access to transmit raw image data exclusively through user-initiated ROS2 topics. No images are stored or processed locally.
- **Location:** GPS data streaming limited to active ROS2 sessions using user-activated ROS2 commands. Location information is never persisted on the device.
- **Fingerprint/Face ID:** Biometric authentication for ROS2-secured operations initiated through explicit user interaction. Biometric templates never leave the device and are not transmitted.
- **Network:** Local network access restricted to ROS2 node communication within user-configured private networks.
