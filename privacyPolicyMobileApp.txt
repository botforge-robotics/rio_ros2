**Last Updated:** [Current Date]

### **1. Introduction**
Welcome to ROS2Sense ("we," "our," or "us"). This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application ROS2Sense (the "App").

---

### **2. Information We Collect**

#### **2.1 Sensor Data**
Our app collects data from various device sensors, including:
- Accelerometer
- Gyroscope
- Magnetometer
- GPS/Location
- Ambient Light Sensor
- Pressure Sensor
- Temperature Sensor
- Humidity Sensor
- Camera
- Flash
- Microphone
- Speaker
- Fingerprint/Face ID
- SMS

#### **2.2 How We Use Sensor Data**
- All sensor data is processed locally on your device.
- Data is used for ROS2 (Robot Operating System 2) communication.
- No sensor data is stored permanently on your device.
- No sensor data is transmitted to external servers except when explicitly sharing with ROS2 nodes.
- **Camera** access is used only for computer vision processing.
- **GPS** location is used only for robotics applications.
- **Fingerprint/Face ID** is used for authentication and security purposes within robotics applications.
- **SMS** permission is used to allow the app to send automated messages based on ROS2 actions.

---

### **3. Data Storage and Transmission**

#### **3.1 Local Storage**
- The app stores minimal configuration data locally.
- No sensitive personal information is stored.
- All sensor readings are temporary and cleared when the app is closed.

#### **3.2 Data Transmission**
- Data is only transmitted to ROS2 nodes on your local network.
- No data is sent to our servers.
- All network communication is confined to your local network.
- We do not collect or transmit any personal information.

---

### **4. Permissions**
The app requires the following permissions:
- **Camera:** For computer vision processing.
- **Location:** For GPS sensor data.
- **Fingerprint/Face ID:** For authentication and security.
- **Sensors:** For accessing device sensors.
- **Network:** For ROS2 communication.
- **SMS:** To send messages based on ROS2 actions.
